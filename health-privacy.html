Ryan Health privacy notice

Updated October 27, 2026

Ryan Health is a private tracker for its owner's daily health journal and WHOOP metrics. It is not a public coaching service.

Information the tracker stores

When the owner connects WHOOP, the tracker reads authorized recovery, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, cycle, strain, steps when available, and workout information. It also stores the owner's daily answers and optional notes. ChatGPT sign-in identifies the owner and protects access.

How information is used

The information supports daily logs, history, charts, and exports requested by the owner. Health records are kept separately from Focus. Focus links to this tracker and does not receive its health records. The research catalog does not automatically prescribe treatments.

Storage and service providers

The tracker runs on OpenAI Sites with Cloudflare server and database infrastructure. These providers process information to host and operate the tracker. WHOOP supplies information after the owner grants access. WHOOP credentials remain on the server; stored access and refresh tokens are encrypted. No health records are intentionally sent to design or code-review models.

Sharing

The tracker is configured for owner-only access. It does not sell health records or share them with a coach. Exports are created only when the owner requests them. This public notice contains no health records.

Retention and control

Saved logs and imported history remain until the owner removes them through database maintenance. Disconnecting WHOOP stops the connection and retains existing history; it does not delete that history. Access can also be revoked through WHOOP. The owner can request removal of stored history through the development workspace used to maintain the tracker.

Questions or corrections should be directed to the owner of Ryan Health through the app's registered WHOOP contact.
